How to download a Microsoft Teams transcript

Updated 31 October 2026 · TXT, Word (.docx), SubRip (.srt) and WebVTT (.vtt) · Türkçe

You finish a long Teams meeting, someone says "can you send me the notes?", and you realise the only thing you really need is the transcript. I run into this all the time, so here is exactly how I get it, step by step. There are two ways: the built-in one, and the one I use when the built-in one doesn't let me download.

The short version

  • Transcription has to be switched on during the meeting. If it wasn't, there is nothing to download, sorry.
  • Fastest route: Teams → Chat → the past meeting → Recap → Transcript → Download → .docx or .vtt.
  • If you can read the transcript but there's no Download button, open the recording page and use Transcript Saver for TXT, Word or SRT.

Method 1: Download it from the Recap tab

This is the official way, and it takes under a minute.

  1. Open Microsoft Teams and click Chat. You can also go through Calendar, whichever you find faster.
  2. Open the chat (or calendar event) of the meeting that just ended.
  3. Click the Recap tab, then Transcript.
  4. Click the little arrow next to Download and pick .docx or .vtt.

Why can't I download the Teams transcript?

That's the part that trips most people up. By default only the meeting organizer and co-organizers can download the transcript. Everyone else in the organization can often read it but not download it, and external guests usually can't see it at all. The organizer and your IT admin decide this, not you, so the first thing to try is asking the organizer to download it or to change the setting. And if the Recap tab looks empty, give it a few minutes after the meeting ends.

Method 2: Save it from the recording page with Transcript Saver

I built Transcript Saver for exactly the "I can see the transcript but can't grab it" situation. When you open a meeting recording in the Microsoft Stream / SharePoint player, the transcript sits in a panel next to the video. The extension adds a small toolbar to that page and turns whatever the panel shows into a file.

  1. Install Transcript Saver from the Chrome Web Store or Microsoft Edge Add-ons.
  2. Open the meeting recording, from Recap, from the chat, or from a link someone shared. The address looks like …sharepoint.com/…/stream.aspx.
  3. Open the Transcript panel next to the video.
  4. Click TXT, Word, SRT or Copy in the toolbar.

After you click, the extension scrolls through the transcript panel so every line gets loaded, reads the text, and saves the file to your normal download folder. Let it finish before you switch tabs. A long meeting can take a few seconds.

About privacy: it only reads the page when you click a button, keeps nothing after you leave the page, and sends nothing anywhere. I wrote it all down in the privacy policy.

If something doesn't work

  • There's no transcript at all. Transcription wasn't started in that meeting, and Teams can't create it afterwards.
  • I can't see the toolbar. It only shows up on recording player pages (stream.aspx), not inside the Teams chat window. Open the recording from Recap and refresh the page.
  • The file is shorter than expected. Keep the transcript panel open and wait for the scrolling to finish before you switch tabs.
  • The meeting was sensitive. Please follow your organization's rules about sharing transcripts and recordings. A download button that is hidden is hidden for a reason.
